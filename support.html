Island Owl support

Island Owl is made by David Oreper. For a question or a problem, email islandowlsupport@gmail.com. Please say which device and iOS version you use. I read every message and usually reply within a few days.

You can also open an issue at github.com/doreper/islandowl-site/issues; issues are public, so leave personal details out.

Common questions

How do I mute the sound?

Tap the speaker button in the top left corner. Tap it again to turn the sound back on.

How do I choose a song?

Touch and hold the speaker button. The menu has Next song, Play all songs or Repeat this song, and the list of twelve songs.

How do I switch between light and dark?

Touch and hold the speaker button and choose System, Light or Dark under Appearance.

Why is it landscape only?

The island is drawn as a wide scene, so the app stays in landscape. Turn your device sideways.

Do I need an account, and does the app collect anything?

No. There is no account, no network connection, no ads and no tracking. The app stores only your mute, song and appearance choices on your device, and deleting the app removes them. See the privacy policy.
