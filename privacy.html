Island Owl privacy policy

Last updated October 3, 2026.

Island Owl does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network connections.

The app keeps a few settings on your device only: whether sound is muted, which song is chosen, whether one song repeats, and whether you picked System, Light or Dark appearance. These settings never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

The app uses the date and time on your device to decide what the island looks like. It does not use your location.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/doreper/islandowl-site/issues. Issues are public, so please leave personal details out.
